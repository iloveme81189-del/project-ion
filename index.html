About Project.ION

Founded by Dr. Hari Krishna Budagala, Project.ION is dedicated to bridging academic and industry standards for students in M Pharm, B Pharm, Pharm D, Life Sciences, and PhD programs. We provide personalized, expert guidance for project selection, AI-powered research, and report preparation—empowering students to innovate, learn, and excel in their pharmaceutical and life sciences careers.

Projects done through us benefit from our commitment to assisting in publication in top-tier journals with great indexes.
