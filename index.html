About Project.ION

Dr. Hari Krishna Budagala is a graduate of Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) and holds advanced expertise in pharmaceutical sciences with extensive experience in academia and industry. He specializes in clinical pharmacy, and the application of AI-driven methodologies to enhance pharmaceutical research.

With a strong commitment to student success, Dr. Hari Krishna brings hands-on guidance in research project design, data analysis, and publication support for PharmD, M Pharm, B Pharm, Life Sciences, and PhD students. His dedication to integrating practical industry knowledge and cutting-edge AI tools empowers students to innovate and excel in their academic and professional pursuits.

As the founder of Project.ION, he fosters a supportive environment where students receive personalized assistance aimed at bridging academic knowledge and real-world pharmaceutical practice.

Projects done through us benefit from our commitment to assisting in publication in top-tier journals with great indexes.
